# GovGuide Launch Posts

## Reddit: r/india

**Title**: I built a free guide to 137 Indian government services — requirements, fees, and step-by-step procedures in one place

**Body**:

Every time I needed to apply for a government document, I'd spend hours across different websites trying to figure out the exact requirements, fees, and procedures. Different sites had conflicting info, and I never knew what was actually current.

So I built GovGuide — a free, open-source tool that covers 137+ government services with verified requirements, fees, processing times, and direct links to official portals.

**What it covers:**
- Identity docs (Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, Voter ID, Driving License)
- Business registrations (GST, MSME Udyam, Company/LLP, FSSAI)
- Certificates (Birth, Death, Caste, Income, Domicile, Marriage)
- Social welfare schemes (PM Kisan, Ayushman Bharat, PM Awas, e-Shram)
- Property services, pensions, insurance, and more

**What makes it different:**
- Every requirement is traced to its official government source
- It tells you exactly what documents you need, what it costs, and how long it takes
- It does NOT collect your Aadhaar, passwords, or personal data
- It's an informational guide, not an application portal
